## Final data flow and invariants

1. The browser follows the observed company-search UI, selects company 8, clears
   all search checkboxes and chooses only Kokurakita, submits search, and clicks
   the area/property print link. It captures the request actually caused by that
   click; no PDF endpoint is fabricated. Login/DOM failure never becomes zero.
2. Original PDFs and acquisition evidence remain private. Parser QC and exact
   search-result count, area, rent/area validity, room identity and canonical
   matching are mandatory. Initial automatic seeding remains off. Unmatched or
   suspect rows stop the disposable update; they are not guessed into buildings.
3. Provider stays `ulucks`; snapshot source is a company/area scope hash. Historical
   listings, raw evidence, building-source mappings, aliases and source priorities
   are checked for preservation. Existing canonical IDs/names/addresses and other
   current snapshot pointers are checked independently. No legacy ownership keys
   are claimed. Exact overlap suppression affects aggregation, not stored history.
4. Rental priority is **per canonical building**, before aggregating all fields:
   Ulucks, then RealPro, then Mansion Review rental. The existing compatibility
   behavior combines legacy rows if any provider has no explicit rank; it is not
   silently converted into item-level or field-level precedence. Building
   attributes use selected rental facts and canonical fallbacks, not an independent
   per-field provider merge. That distinction remains explicit for future sources.
5. Sales retain their separate tables and aggregation policy. This work does not
   acquire sales or change their historical selection rules. Existing sale rows
   and rental/sale links remain protected; source-specific sale freshness needs
   its own contract before the future sales collector is enabled.
6. A pre-update private backup precedes collection. Immutable master/public objects
   are uploaded before the single generation-checked `state/current.json` commit.
   Failure or a concurrent winner cannot replace the previous validated head.
   Public export copies only approved aggregate tables, including no listings,
   raw data, original PDF or unit identifiers.
7. Pages downloads only the generation-pinned public object, checks its hash,
   schema and counts, and renders the existing templates/URLs. Release proof now
   checks fees, areas and layouts as well as vacancies and rent in production JSON.
   Up to three affected HTML pages are fetched and checked for building identity
   and displayed vacancy/rent; JSON success alone cannot pass a broken detail page.
   The weekly schedule is Monday 04:00 Asia/Tokyo, enabled after production proof.
   Scheduler configuration success is distinct from a later unattended execution.

## Evidence boundaries and remaining work

Local synthetic tests, Chromium navigation, a real-reference parser check and a
container smoke test are separate evidence categories. They do not establish
access to the existing private master, actual GCP Job configuration, live Ulucks
login, a new PDF, a GCS release, or production data publication. Those results must
come from the authenticated first activation and its private evidence file.

`bash cloud/complete_initial_release.sh FULL_REVIEWED_MAIN_SHA [EXISTING_CHECKOUT]`
is the single prepared-Cloud-Shell entrypoint. It does not provision identities,
recreate infrastructure, modify Secrets or regenerate a master from public data.
The verified master/review already in the existing checkout are required. A real
ambiguous overlap, unmatched building, missing access or conflicting existing
resource still stops safely and records the reason; tests cannot waive it.

RealPro and Mansion Review integration still require live adapters, separate
rental/sales scopes, verified legacy ownership boundaries, full source field/QC
contracts, source-specific failure/drop policies, and live-to-production proofs.
The Ulucks collector deliberately rejects other targets until those are built.
